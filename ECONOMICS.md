# Unit Economics

## Lead Value Estimation

Assume:
- 1 in 10 consultations converts
- Average customer generates $3,000 annual revenue
- Consultation close rate = 10%

Expected value per booked consultation:

$3,000 × 10% = $300

If 20% of audit users book consultations:

Expected value per audit:
$300 × 20% = $60

This means each qualified audit lead could reasonably be worth $40–$60.

## What Must Be True To Reach $1M ARR

Assume:
- Average annual revenue per customer = $3,000
- Target ARR = $1,000,000

Needed customers:

1,000,000 / 3,000 = ~333 customers

If:
- consultation conversion = 10%
- audit-to-consultation rate = 20%

Then:
333 customers requires roughly:

16,650 audit completions.

Over 18 months:
~925 audits/month.

This is realistic with:
- SEO
- Product Hunt
- social distribution
- startup partnerships
