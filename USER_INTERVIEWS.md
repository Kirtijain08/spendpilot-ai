# User Interview 1

## Person
A.K. — Startup Founder — 8 person SaaS startup

## Quotes
- “We signed up for AI tools too quickly.”
- “Nobody on the team knows what we’re actually paying monthly.”
- “I’d absolutely use a free audit tool.”

## Most Surprising Insight
They cared more about visibility than pure savings.

## Impact On Product
Added:
- spend comparison visuals
- optimization score
- cleaner reporting UX
